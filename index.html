Operation Blue Room

Operation Blue Room was an initiative carried out by my team to disrupt the activities of an online group known as "Red Room". Its members were reportedly involved in distributing child sexual abuse material, extortion, and hate speech targeting minorities.

Reported Outcomes

  • Seven alleged cybercriminals reportedly taken down or arrested.
  • Two team accounts lost during the operation.

These figures reflect the team's reported outcomes and should not be interpreted as independently verified results.
